Our Story

15+ Years of Quality Construction & Remodeling

Torres Interior & Exterior Services was founded on a simple idea: homeowners and businesses deserve a contractor who shows up on time, communicates clearly, and does the job right the first time.

What started as a small residential painting and repair crew has grown into a full-service construction company handling interior remodels, exterior renovations, and general contracting for clients across the region — without ever losing that hands-on, family-owned approach.

Today, our licensed and insured team has completed more than 500 projects, from single-room updates to full property renovations, and we're just getting started.
