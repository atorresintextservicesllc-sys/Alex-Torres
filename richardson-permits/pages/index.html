How it works

Four steps from "send it over" to "issued"

  1. Send us the job

    Address, scope and whatever documents you have. A text or email works.

  2. We prep & submit

    We check the package against the municipality's requirements, fill any gaps, then submit.

  3. We clear comments

    We track review, relay comments to you and your designer, and resubmit fast.

  4. Permit issued

    You get the permit card and we can schedule your inspections.
